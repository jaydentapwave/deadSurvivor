NO text, NO icon. 9-slice layout: border is exactly 4 blocks thick on all four sides; the four 4x4 corner squares may hold a rivet; the edges between the corners repeat one short pattern evenly along the whole length; the centre 24x8 blocks are ONE flat colour.
