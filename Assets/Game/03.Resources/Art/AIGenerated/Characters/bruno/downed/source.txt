## 최종 프롬프트 (설정 A, Codex에 보낸 그대로의 이미지 프롬프트 부분)
The character and the art style come from the first image; the pose comes from the second image. The first image is a 64x64 pixel sprite of a warrior enlarged so every pixel is one 16x16 flat block (canvas 1024x1024). The second image is a rough colour mannequin (same scale, same block grid) showing the SAME warrior collapsed, lying face-down on the ground, defeated. Draw the warrior from the first image in exactly the pose of the second image: lying flat on his front, body horizontal, head on the RIGHT side resting face-down on the ground (we see mostly his spiky red hair), legs stretched to the LEFT with both brown boots visible at the left end, the big brown backpack with the rolled bedroll still strapped ON HIS BACK and seen on top of the body (not in front of him, not beside him), the red cape lying over his legs, one arm reaching forward to the right on the ground, the straight steel long sword lying flat on the ground just beyond that hand, the round wooden shield with a gold boss lying on the ground by his side.
Size: keep the same 16x16 block grid. The lying figure from boot soles to top of hair is about 42 blocks long and the whole picture including the sword is at most 48 blocks wide and about 22-26 blocks tall, sitting at the bottom of the canvas, horizontally centred.
Colours and details from the first image: spiky red hair (#c44334, shading #77211d), red scarf, neutral grey steel armor (#c8b8b8 #a89898 #786868, not beige), brown leather and boots (#af642e), red tabard and cape.
Pixel rules: every block is ONE flat colour; no detail smaller than one block; no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no soft edges. A 1-block dark brown outline (#34140e, not black) around the whole figure, the sword and the shield. Flat 2-3 tone shading.
Background: flat solid #ff00ff everywhere outside the character. No grid lines, no text, no UI, no ground, no cast shadow.
