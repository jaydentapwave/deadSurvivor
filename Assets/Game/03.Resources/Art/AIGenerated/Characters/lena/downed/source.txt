## 최종 프롬프트 (설정 A, 이미지 프롬프트 부분)
The character and the art style come from the first image; the pose comes from the second image. The first image is a 64x64 pixel sprite of an elf archer girl enlarged so every pixel is one 16x16 flat block (canvas 1024x1024). The second image is a rough colour mannequin (same scale, same block grid) showing the SAME archer collapsed, lying face-down on the ground, defeated. Draw the archer from the first image in exactly the pose of the second image: lying flat on her front, body horizontal, head on the RIGHT side resting face-down on the ground (we see mostly her green hood with a little light-brown hair at the front, no face), legs stretched to the LEFT with both brown boots visible at the left end, the big brown backpack still strapped ON HER BACK and seen on top of the body (not in front of her, not beside her), the quiver with white-feathered arrows also on her back next to the backpack, the green cape lying over her back and legs, one arm reaching forward to the right on the ground, the wooden short bow with its string lying flat on the ground just beyond that hand.
Size: keep the same 16x16 block grid. The lying figure from boot soles to top of hood is about 42 blocks long and the whole picture including the bow is at most 50 blocks wide and about 20-24 blocks tall, sitting at the bottom of the canvas, horizontally centred.
Colours and details from the first image: green hood and cape (#4f7a2e, light #7fa040, dark #3a3721), light-brown hair (#d5833a), skin (#f8daac), brown leather backpack and bow (#af642e, dark #774c3b), dark trousers, dark red-brown boots (#77211d), white arrow feathers.
Pixel rules: every block is ONE flat colour; no detail smaller than one block; no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no soft edges. A 1-block very dark brown outline (#2f1108, not black) around the whole figure and the bow. Flat 2-3 tone shading.
Background: flat solid #ff00ff everywhere outside the character. No grid lines, no text, no UI, no ground, no cast shadow.
