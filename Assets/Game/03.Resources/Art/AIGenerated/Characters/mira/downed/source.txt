## 최종 프롬프트 (설정 A, 이미지 프롬프트 부분)
The character and the art style come from the first image; the pose comes from the second image. The first image is a 64x64 pixel sprite of a young mage girl enlarged so every pixel is one 16x16 flat block (canvas 1024x1024). The second image is a rough colour mannequin (same scale, same block grid) showing the SAME mage collapsed, lying face-down on the ground, defeated. Draw the mage from the first image in exactly the pose of the second image: lying flat on her front, body horizontal, head on the RIGHT side resting face-down on the ground (we see mostly her long silver-grey hair spread on the ground, no face), her big blue pointed witch hat with the gold star still on her head but knocked askew, its tip pointing up and to the right, legs stretched to the LEFT with both brown boots visible at the left end, the blue robe with gold trim covering her body and legs with a little of the cream dress at the hem, the big brown backpack with scrolls and books sticking out still strapped ON HER BACK and seen on top of the body (not in front of her, not beside her); the backpack is a rounded soft leather bag, no lock, no two light spots side by side, one arm reaching forward to the right on the ground, the wooden staff with the glowing cyan orb lying flat on the ground just beyond that hand, orb end at the right.
Size: keep the same 16x16 block grid. The lying figure from boot soles to the top of the head is about 42 blocks long and the whole picture including the staff and hat is at most 50 blocks wide and about 20-26 blocks tall, sitting at the bottom of the canvas, horizontally centred.
Colours and details from the first image: blue hat and robe (#40548b, dark #2b3566), gold trim and star (#e4b87d), silver-grey hair (#c8b8b8, shade #a89898), skin (#f8daac), cream dress (#e3d7c8), brown leather backpack and staff (#af642e, dark #774c3b), cyan orb (#7fd0c8), dark brown boots.
Pixel rules: every block is ONE flat colour; no detail smaller than one block; no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no soft edges. A 1-block very dark brown outline (#34140e, not black) around the whole figure, the hat and the staff. Flat 2-3 tone shading.
Background: flat solid #ff00ff everywhere outside the character. No grid lines, no text, no UI, no ground, no cast shadow, no sparkles.
